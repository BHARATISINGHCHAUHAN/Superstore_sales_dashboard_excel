# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Superstore Sales Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Excel**. The dashboard provides meaningful business insights by analyzing sales, profit, customer segments, categories, and regional performance.

The project includes **data cleaning, KPI creation, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and dashboard design** to transform raw data into an interactive business report.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze Superstore sales performance.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard.
- Identify sales and profit trends.
- Generate business insights for decision-making.

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- 📈 Overall sales show a positive growth trend from **2011–2014**, indicating steady business growth.
- 💻 **Technology** is the highest-performing category in terms of both **Sales and Profit**.
- 👥 The **Consumer** segment contributes the largest share of total sales (around **52%**), followed by Corporate and Home Office.
- 📅 Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, but the overall trend remains upward.
- 🌍 Regional sales vary significantly, highlighting differences in market performance across regions.
